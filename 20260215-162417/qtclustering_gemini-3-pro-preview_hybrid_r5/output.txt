The `qtclustering` benchmark has been parallelized using a hybrid approach combining MPI, OpenMP, and CUDA:

1.  **MPI (Distributed Memory)**: The main workload—finding the best seed point among all unclustered points—is distributed across MPI ranks. Each rank processes a subset of the unclustered points. `MPI_Allreduce` is used to identify the globally optimal seed with the maximum cluster cardinality. The winning rank broadcasts the seed to all other ranks, ensuring all processes maintain a consistent state of the clustering process.

2.  **CUDA (GPU Acceleration)**: The computationally intensive task of generating candidate clusters and determining their size for each seed is offloaded to the GPU. A custom CUDA kernel, `compute_cluster_sizes_kernel`, processes multiple seeds in parallel.
