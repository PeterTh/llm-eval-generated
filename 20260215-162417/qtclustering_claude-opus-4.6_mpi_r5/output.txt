**qtclustering.cpp**: 
- Added `#include <mpi.h>` and `#include <climits>`
- **`qtClustering()`**: Takes `rank` and `num_procs` parameters. The seed evaluation loop is partitioned round-robin across MPI ranks. After each rank finds its local best candidate cluster, three MPI collectives synchronize the global result: `MPI_Allreduce(MAX)` for max cardinality, `MPI_Allreduce(MINLOC)` for tie-breaking on smallest seed index, and `MPI_Bcast` to distribute the winning cluster's members.
- **`main()`**: Added `MPI_Init`/`MPI_Finalize`, `MPI_Barrier` around timing, and all I/O is gated on rank 0.
