The parallelized N-body benchmark is complete. Here's a summary of the hybrid MPI + OpenMP + CUDA approach:

## Architecture

**MPI (Inter-node/Rank distribution):**
- Bodies are block-distributed across MPI ranks
- `MPI_Allgatherv` exchanges all body positions to every rank at each step (needed for O(N²) force computation)
- `MPI_Gatherv` gathers results to rank 0 for output
- `MPI_Reduce` aggregates energy and validation results

**CUDA (GPU acceleration):**
- Force computation (the O(N²) bottleneck) offloaded to GPU via `computeForcesKernel`
- Velocity update + position integration via `integrateKernel`
- Each thread handles one local body with sequential accumulation over all bodies (bit-exact reproducibility)
- Async memory transfers via CUDA streams
- Round-robin GPU assignment across ranks

**OpenMP (CPU parallelism):**
- Parallel validation of local bodies
- Parallel kinetic energy computation
- Parallel potential energy computation
