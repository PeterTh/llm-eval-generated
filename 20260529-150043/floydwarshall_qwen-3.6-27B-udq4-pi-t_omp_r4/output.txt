All done. Here's a summary of the parallelization changes:

### `floydwarshall.cpp`
- **Added `#include <omp.h>`** for OpenMP support
- **`initializeDistanceMatrix`**: Parallelized with `#pragma omp parallel for schedule(static)` using per-element seeds for thread-safe random generation
- **`initializePathMatrix`**: Parallelized with `#pragma omp parallel for schedule(static)` on the outer loop
- **`floydWarshall`** (main kernel):
  - `k` loop remains sequential (required for correctness — each k-iteration depends on k-1)
  - `i` loop parallelized with `#pragma omp parallel for schedule(static)` — distributes rows across threads
  - `j` loop vectorized with `#pragma omp simd` — enables SIMD auto-vectorization of the innermost loop
  - `distIK` hoisted outside the j-loop (invariant, already was)
- **`validateResult`**: Parallelized diagonal check and triangle inequality check (`collapse(2)`) with flag-based early exit (avoids structured block violation)

### `CMakeLists.txt`
- Added `find_package(OpenMP REQUIRED)`
- Linked `OpenMP::OpenMP_CXX` to the target
