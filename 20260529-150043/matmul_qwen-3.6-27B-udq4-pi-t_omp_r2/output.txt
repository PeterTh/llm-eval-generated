### `matmul/matmul.cpp`
1. **Added `#include <omp.h>`** for OpenMP support
2. **`initMatrix`**: Parallelized with `#pragma omp parallel for collapse(2) schedule(static)` — each element is independent
3. **`matrixMultiply`**: Replaced the naive triple-nested loop with a **3D tiled/loop-blocked** parallel version:
   - **Tile size = 64** for L1/L2 cache efficiency
   - **`collapse(2)`** on the `(ii, jj)` tile loops distributes unique output tiles across threads — each `C[i*N+j]` is written by exactly one thread (no races)
   - **K-tile accumulation** improves reuse of A and B sub-blocks in cache
   - **`schedule(static)`** for uniform workload distribution (no overhead of dynamic scheduling)
   - Hoisted `i * N` into `iN` to avoid redundant multiplication

### `matmul/CMakeLists.txt`

2. Changed `target_link_libraries` to link `OpenMP::OpenMP_CXX` instead of just `m`
