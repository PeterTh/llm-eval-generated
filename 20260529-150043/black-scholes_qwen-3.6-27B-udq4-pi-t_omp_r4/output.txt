### Key design choices
- **`schedule(static)`** — optimal for this workload since all iterations have equal computational cost, giving the best load balance with minimal overhead
- **`#pragma omp parallel for`** — the most straightforward and efficient OpenMP construct for this embarrassingly parallel pattern (each option is priced independently with no data dependencies)
