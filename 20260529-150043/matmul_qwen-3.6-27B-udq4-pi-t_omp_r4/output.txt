### `matmul/matmul.cpp` — OpenMP parallelization with cache optimization

1. **`#include <omp.h>`** added

2. **`initMatrix`** — Parallelized with `#pragma omp parallel for collapse(2) schedule(static)` across both i,j loops

3. **`matrixMultiply`** — Major rewrite with two key optimizations:
   - **Loop tiling (blocking)** with `BLOCK=64` for cache efficiency: outer tile loops `(ii, jj)` are parallelized with `collapse(2) schedule(static)`, distributing tile pairs across threads
   - **i-k-j inner loop ordering**: A is accessed row-wise, B is accessed row-wise (not column-wise), and C is accumulated. This gives far better cache behavior than the original i-j-k order
   - **`#pragma omp simd`** on the innermost j-loop for vectorization
   - Parallel C initialization (clear to 0.0)

4. **`validateResult`** — Parallelized with `collapse(2)` and thread-safe `#pragma omp critical` for error printing

5. **Thread count display** — Shows `omp_get_max_threads()` in output
