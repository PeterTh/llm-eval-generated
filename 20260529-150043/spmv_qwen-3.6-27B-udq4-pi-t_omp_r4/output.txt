The SpMV benchmark has been parallelized with OpenMP. Here's a summary of the changes:

### `spmv/spmv.cpp` — 4 changes:

1. **Added `#include <omp.h>`** for OpenMP support.

2. **`spmvCpu` (main kernel)** — Parallelized with:
   - `#pragma omp parallel for schedule(static)` on the outer loop (each row is independent)
   - `#pragma omp simd reduction(+:t)` on the inner dot-product loop for SIMD vectorization

3. **`fill`** — Parallelized with `#pragma omp parallel for schedule(static)` for faster initialization.

4. **`verifyResults`** — Parallelized with `#pragma omp parallel for schedule(static)`, refactored to use a `valid` flag instead of early `return false` (early return breaks parallel correctness).

### `spmv/CMakeLists.txt` — 2 changes:

- Added `find_package(OpenMP REQUIRED)`
- Linked `OpenMP::OpenMP_CXX` to the target
