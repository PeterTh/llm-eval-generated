### Parallelization strategy

- **`collapse(3)`** distributes work across all three nested loops for better load balancing and cache utilization on large grids
- **`schedule(static)`** provides deterministic chunk distribution with minimal overhead, ideal for uniform work per iteration
- The time-step loop remains sequential (each step depends on the previous), which is correct for the Cahn-Hilliard scheme
