### `floydwarshall.cpp`
- Added `#include <omp.h>` for OpenMP support
- Parallelized the `floydWarshall` function's inner `i` loop with `#pragma omp parallel for schedule(static)`:
  - The outer `k` loop remains sequential (data dependency between iterations)
  - The `i` loop is parallelized — each thread works on independent rows, so no race conditions exist
  - `dist[i][k]` is hoisted out of the `j` loop as a local variable (invariant for fixed `i,k`)
  - `dist[i][k]` and `dist[k][j]` are read-only during iteration `k` (they never change because `dist[i][k] = min(dist[i][k], dist[i][k]+0)`)
  - `schedule(static)` gives even work distribution across threads
