- Parallelized independent seed-cluster generation across CPU cores.
- Preserved deterministic serial tie-breaking and equivalent results.
- Reduced candidate construction from cubic to quadratic work by caching maximum distances.
- Reused per-thread scratch buffers to reduce allocation overhead.
- Made OpenMP a required, unconditional build dependency.
- Kept the executable name `qtclustering`.
