I’m restructuring it as row-distributed CSR: MPI partitions rows across ranks, each rank keeps the full input vector but only its matrix slice, OpenMP handles host-side partition/index work, and CUDA runs the local SpMV with one warp per row. This avoids inter-rank communication inside the timed iterations.
