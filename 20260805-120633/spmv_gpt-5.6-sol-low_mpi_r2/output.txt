Implemented MPI distributed-memory parallelism in `spmv` without adding files or changing the executable name.

Key changes:

- CSR matrix rows distributed across MPI ranks.
- Work partitioned by nonzero count for improved load balance.
- Dense input vector broadcast once to all ranks.
- Local SpMV computation requires no communication in the timed loop.
- Results gathered once after all iterations.
- Runtime uses the slowest rank’s elapsed time for accurate scalability metrics.
- Initialization, reporting, validation, and result printing remain root-only.
- CMake now requires and links MPI.

Validation completed successfully with:

- 4 ranks on a 128×128 matrix.
- 6 ranks on a 3×3 matrix, covering ranks with no assigned rows.
